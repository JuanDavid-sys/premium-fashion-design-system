The Art of Layering: A Guide to Transitional Dressing

Sarah Chen, Creative Director August 10, 2026 5 min read

There's a moment each year when the morning air carries a subtle chill, the light shifts to something golden, and you instinctively reach for an extra layer. Transitional dressing — the art of navigating between seasons — is where true style intelligence reveals itself. It's not about simply adding a jacket; it's about composing outfits that breathe, adapt, and evolve throughout the day.

At ATELIER, we design with this rhythm in mind. Our collections are built to be mixed, matched, and layered — creating a wardrobe that responds to your life, not just the forecast. Here's how to master the art of the in-between.

Start with a Strong Foundation

Every great layered look begins with a considered base. A fitted merino turtleneck in charcoal or cream serves as the perfect starting point — warm enough for brisk mornings, light enough to wear under a blazer when the afternoon sun breaks through. Our Merino Turtleneck ($195) is crafted from extra-fine merino wool that regulates temperature naturally, making it the ultimate transitional staple.

Pair it with our Linen Trousers ($225) for a combination that balances structure with ease. The linen's natural breathability prevents overheating, while the tailored silhouette keeps everything polished.

"True luxury is the ability to move through the world with confidence — and that starts with dressing for the moment you're in, not the one you left."

— James Whitford, Creative Director

The Middle Layer: Your Secret Weapon

The middle layer is where the magic happens. It provides warmth, texture, and visual depth. A Cashmere Sweater ($295) draped over your shoulders or tied loosely at the waist adds an effortless sophistication that signals you understand the nuances of dressing well.

For a more structured approach, try our Tailored Blazer ($385) over a simple cotton shirt. The blazer's clean lines create a polished silhouette, while the shirt underneath keeps the look relaxed. This combination transitions seamlessly from a morning meeting to an evening dinner.

The Outer Layer: Make a Statement

Your outer layer sets the tone for the entire look. A Wool Coat ($495) in camel or black is the investment piece that ties everything together. It's the first thing people see and the last thing you take off — so make it count.

For days when you need something more versatile, our Leather Jacket ($595) adds edge without sacrificing elegance. Hand-finished from premium Italian leather, it develops a unique patina over time, becoming more characterful with each wear.

The key to transitional layering is proportion. A longer coat over a cropped knit, a blazer over a flowing blouse, a structured jacket over relaxed trousers — it's the contrast between fitted and flowing, structured and soft, that creates visual interest.

Accessories: The Finishing Touch

No layered look is complete without thoughtful accessories. A lightweight scarf in silk or cashmere adds warmth without bulk. A structured leather bag grounds the outfit. And the right pair of sunglasses — because transitional seasons often mean bright skies and cool air simultaneously.

Remember, the goal of transitional dressing isn't just to stay comfortable. It's to express intention. When you layer with purpose, every outfit becomes a statement about who you are and how you move through the world.
