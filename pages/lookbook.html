The Vision

Autumn/Winter 2026

This season, we explore the intersection of structure and softness. The AW26 collection draws inspiration from architectural forms — clean lines, bold silhouettes, and a palette that mirrors the urban landscape in autumn light.

Each piece is designed to be layered, mixed, and reimagined. From oversized wool coats that envelop you in warmth to silk blouses that catch the light with every movement, this collection is about confidence, comfort, and the quiet luxury of exceptional materials.

We've expanded our use of certified organic cashmere and introduced new fabrications in recycled wool and plant-based leather alternatives — proving that sustainability and luxury are not mutually exclusive.
