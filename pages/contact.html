Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions

We offer complimentary standard shipping on all orders over $300. Standard delivery takes 5-7 business days. Express shipping (2-3 business days) is available for $15, and next-day delivery is available for $25. International shipping is available to select countries.
We accept returns within 30 days of delivery. Items must be unworn with original tags attached. To initiate a return, visit our Returns portal or contact our customer service team. Exchanges are subject to availability.
Yes, we offer complimentary hem adjustments on trousers and skirts purchased from ATELIER. For other alteration requests, please contact our customer service team or visit one of our boutiques for a personal fitting consultation.
Each product page includes a detailed size guide with measurements. We recommend taking your measurements and comparing them to our size chart. If you're between sizes, we generally suggest sizing up for a relaxed fit. Our customer service team is also available to help with sizing recommendations.
Sustainability is at the core of everything we do. We use certified organic and recycled materials wherever possible, partner with factories that uphold fair labor practices, and offset 100% of our shipping emissions. Learn more on our Sustainability page.
